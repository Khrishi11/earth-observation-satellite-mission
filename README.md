# Earth Observation Satellite Mission: Plastic Pollution

Space Engineering (UFMF8V-15-3), UWE Bristol (2024). Design of a small satellite mission to monitor plastic pollution around Shanghai.

## What was covered
- **Orbital mechanics:** region of interest, payload selection, orbit trade off, ground station location and field of view
- **Platform stability:** attitude and orbit control subsystem (AOCS), attitude knowledge and accuracy, disturbance sources
- **Communications:** initial link budget calculations and a link budget in STK
